# StackWise

A private medication and supplement tracker for iOS and Android.

- Track what you take, when, and whether you took it.
- Reminders at the times you choose. Works offline.
- Protocols: cycles and step-up dosing schedules.
- Interaction checker built from published sources. Informational only, never medical advice.
- Everything stays on your phone. No account, no cloud copy of your health data.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

This repository hosts the public site: [privacy policy](https://meister5.github.io/stackwise-site/privacy.html), [terms](https://meister5.github.io/stackwise-site/terms.html), and a free [reconstitution calculator](https://meister5.github.io/stackwise-site/calculator.html).
