# StackWise

A private medication and supplement tracker for iOS and Android. Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play.

## Features

- Track what you take, when, and whether you took it
- Reminders at the times you choose, working offline
- Protocols for cycles and step-up dosing schedules
- Interaction checker built from published sources (informational only, not medical advice)
- Everything stays on your phone: no account, no cloud copy of your health data
