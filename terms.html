What StackWise is

StackWise is a personal organizational tool that helps you keep track of the supplements and medications you take, when you take them, and whether you took them. It is designed to help you manage your own routine.

Medical disclaimer — please read this

StackWise is a personal tracking tool designed to help you manage your own supplement and medication schedule. It is not a medical device, does not provide medical advice, and is not a substitute for guidance from a licensed healthcare professional.

Two optional features go a step further and deserve their own callout:

  • The Interaction Checker flags potential concerns between items in your stack for informational purposes only. It is based on a limited, hand-curated reference and may be incomplete or inaccurate. It never tells you a combination is "safe" — it only ever surfaces potential concerns or notes worth discussing with a professional.
  • The Stack Optimizer suggests times of day for your items based on general timing guidance and the same interaction data. Its suggestions are not medical advice and are never a reason to start, stop, or change a prescription on your own.

Always consult your doctor or pharmacist before making changes to any medication or supplement routine, and before relying on anything the app tells you. If a suggestion or flag involves a prescription medication, talk to your pharmacist or doctor before acting on it — don't change a prescription based on the app alone.
