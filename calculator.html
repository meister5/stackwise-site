How it works

Adding water to a vial dilutes the peptide into a concentration — how many milligrams are in each milliliter of liquid. Dividing your desired dose by that concentration tells you how much liquid to draw. Multiplying that volume by 100 gives insulin-syringe units, since a U-100 syringe holds 100 units per milliliter, rounded to the nearest half unit.

concentration (mg/ml) = vial mg ÷ water ml
dose ml = dose mg ÷ concentration
units = dose ml × 100, rounded to nearest 0.5

This is arithmetic, not medical advice. Always confirm doses with your prescriber or pharmacist.
