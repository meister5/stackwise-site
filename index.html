Privacy

Your health data is yours. It stays on your phone.

  • On-device only. Everything you track stays on your phone.
  • No account. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to log into.
  • No cloud copy. Your health data is never uploaded or stored on a server.
  • Optional analytics. Anonymous crash and usage analytics only, with no health content.
